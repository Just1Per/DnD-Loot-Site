Choose the rules for your table

The Adobe character sheet is the normal character view. Its 2014 / 2024 rules button sits at the far right of the Player Name bar on the first page. New sheets default to 2024; existing sheets retain their edition. Campaign DMs control available editions and catalogue choices. A changed source policy preserves saved character choices rather than silently deleting them.

The rules edition, actual level in each class, subclass and sourcebook determine supported effects. A planned subclass becomes active at its required class level. Ordinary multiclass Spellcasting and Warlock Pact Magic have separate slot rules. Artificer has distinct Tasha’s and Eberron: Forge of the Artificer versions; the revised class uses 2024 core rules.

A book’s publication date does not automatically make it a 2024 revision. For example, Monsters of the Multiverse species belong to the 2014-era rules family. Check the edition, book and page on the actual choice, and agree compatibility or homebrew with your DM.

Species, backgrounds, classes and feats

Alongside SRD options, the character catalogue includes published-source references with structured game facts and original short descriptions. Species reminders include inherited traits, such as Trance and Fey Ancestry, within that species’ own description. Supplement options such as Satyr identify their book/version. Full non-SRD book descriptions, artwork and lore are not reproduced.

2014 narrative background features and 2024 backgrounds’ Origin feats are distinct. The feature box offers a short reminder or personal text; editing or clearing it does not replace background training or grant a different feat. Starting equipment and checked starting coins require the equipment preview and confirmation rather than being awarded by the narrative text.

Feature-level references cover 186 selectable subclasses across the catalogue’s thirteen classes, including Artificer. Supported resources, training, spells and other effects depend on the selected source and class level. This is a bounded selection of published options, not a claim to include every subclass ever published or to automate every effect.

The catalogue contains 302 versioned feat references. Structured ability-increase facts cover 178 entries; further supported combat, reaction and resource effects are identified individually. Conditional reminders, such as Savage Attacker or Sentinel, require their actual trigger. Selecting a feat or writing a custom feature does not turn every sentence into an automatic calculation. The 2014 Grappler reminder was corrected against SRD 5.1 page 75: pinning uses another grapple check rather than the saving throw in the upstream transcription.

Some legacy species selections also link to the secondary Wikidot reference. Owlin’s flexible bonuses were checked against the publisher’s Strixhaven preview. The named publication remains the reference for complete rules. Plane Shift and Unearthed Arcana playtest options are not automated.

Spells, short descriptions and additional entries

The spell index contains 969 versioned source records representing 583 spell names: 525 legacy and 444 revised-era records. It includes all 361 PHB (2014) and 391 PHB (2024) spells plus the supplements in the coverage manifest. Source versions keep separate identities.

Full licensed descriptions are provided for 319 SRD 5.1 and 339 SRD 5.2.1 spell records, plus their SRD feats. Non-SRD choices contain factual metadata, source/page citations and original concise reminders, not copied full book entries. The SRD Creative Commons licence does not apply to non-SRD references.

Read spell list groups spells by level and shows casting time, range, components, duration, school and saving throw. Short descriptions include damage, areas and scaling where applicable, with separate facts in the expanded reader. SL means spell slot level, CL character level and SC spellcasting ability modifier. Light radii, target spacing, summoned-creature effects and object dimensions are labelled separately from damage areas.

For example, Hunter’s Mark adds 1d6 per qualifying hit; higher slots extend its duration rather than add damage dice. Fireball starts at 8d6 Fire in a 20-ft-radius sphere and gains 1d6 per slot level above 3. Call Lightning distinguishes the 5-ft radius around each bolt from its larger cloud. Conditional and edition-specific details still require the complete rule.

Choose spells applies each class’s learning or preparation allowance; class, race and feat grants retain their source. A displayed class-list reference does not teach or prepare a spell. Add other spells records permitted off-list catalogue spells or handwritten entries on a separate Additional spells page. It does not grant spell slots, free casts or permission to ignore your table’s rules. Personal short descriptions and full notes remain separate and editable.

Calculations, reminders and table rulings

The engine calculates supported ability bonuses, training, defenses, movement, attacks and resource limits from current character choices. Uses, rests, attunement, spell preparation and other supported controls must be recorded explicitly. Original short reminders explain conditional effects; dice, targets, timing, concentration, exceptions and DM decisions still need review during play.

Aasimar Healing Hands uses an explicit PBd4 roll, recipient and confirmation. Self healing uses the current class/item HP maximum; other-recipient HP stays with the table. Legacy Wild Shape heals the active Beast HP pool, while revised Wild Shape does not retain this species trait. The two sources retain Action versus Magic action, once per Long Rest, and separate private counters. Correcting a use does not reverse healing already applied.

The open-book AI Lookup tool beside the sheet’s zoom/print controls and in supported reference windows offers optional AI-generated explanations and links. A specific reference fills its name, edition and source; the page book also supports manual rule/feature searches. Review the name, edition and source before searching. Results can be wrong and are separate from verified source facts or licensed book text. Searches use Google’s API; the lookup result is not saved as a character or campaign rule.

Supported item spell bonuses retain their source-specific class, ability and alignment scope. Blackstaff requires an explicit record of DM approval in Item information before its non-weapon bonuses apply. Wearing and attunement remain required. Conditional powers, curses and rolled-die changes retain short table instructions.

Numeric spell-description facts support reading and printing. They are separate from the attack engine’s mechanical formulas and do not automatically apply damage to a target. A handwritten spell or item description remains personal text unless a specific supported setting supplies a calculation.

Movement and condition calculations distinguish the two rules editions, including Stunned and Exhaustion. This is not a complete condition automation system. The optional 2014 variant encumbrance is a Player’s Handbook reference (chapter 7), not a default SRD rule. Editable movement text can display a table-specific override without changing the engine’s underlying movement calculation.

Reviewed Ioun ability stones require both attunement and an orbit declaration. The Item information checkbox shares the existing Equipped state. Their +2 ability increase caps at 20 and preserves a higher score; stowing removes the temporary benefit. Ability metadata on books, potions and unreviewed artifacts is a reference, not a permanent award from ownership.

Inventory records owned equipment, wearing, attunement and carriers separately. The magic-item sheet and Attuned Magical Items footer use the same saved ownership and attunement state. Removing a paper page keeps its contents; it does not remove a companion, spend resources or discard spells. Save sheet retains applied character and layout changes.

Reference index and source changes

SRD structured text was obtained from Open5e. Published reference metadata and source checks use the pinned 5etools source revision. These are secondary data tools, not the publisher or a licence to redistribute complete non-SRD books. These tools and this website are not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.

Changes include plain-text conversion of licensed descriptions, matching SRD aliases, explicit source/edition labels, structured filtering and calculations, feature-level references, self-contained original species reminders, and original spell damage/area/scaling reminders. Counts describe the pinned catalogue; they do not promise future publications, homebrew, every adventure reward or complete mechanical automation.

Character-sheet layout reference

The paper layout is based on the supplied MorePurpleMoreBetter (MPMB) 13.0.4 Printer Friendly character sheet. See the MPMB creator’s website for the original project. Layout credits are separate from the SRD rules licences. The web sheet uses CampaignAtlas’s shared character calculations and supported controls.

Creatures and item references

The SRD creature catalogue contains 334 SRD 5.1 entries for 2014 and 341 SRD 5.2.1 entries for 2024, obtained from the D&D 5e SRD API. The SRD Creative Commons attributions below apply to these records. Each retains its edition, source URL, licence and change notice. Statistics are normalized into creature cards and combat snapshots; action text is retained and upstream artwork excluded. Campaign copies retain their source attribution.

Additional item, companion and form references identify their own source and supported facts. A book citation or an original interface summary does not place all material from that book under Creative Commons. Player-uploaded item text, images and personal character notes retain their own ownership and permissions.
