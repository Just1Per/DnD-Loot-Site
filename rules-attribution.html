2024 rules

This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 (“SRD 5.2.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Changes: structured selections, original shortened reminders and automated calculations. New sheets default to 2024; existing sheets retain their edition. 2024 species, four SRD backgrounds and supported class calculations are distinct from the 2014 options. Aasimar and additional Origin feat names are separately sourced PHB references; not all class and feat effects are automated.

Expanded race catalogue

The builder also offers structured game facts and original short reminders for selected published 2014-era supplements. Each selection identifies its book/version and links to the corresponding Wikidot reference. These supplement entries are not claimed to be part of the SRD or covered by its Creative Commons license. Full book descriptions, artwork and lore are not reproduced. Consult the named book for complete rules.

2022 Monsters of the Multiverse entries remain part of the 2014-era rules; they are not the 2024 Player’s Handbook versions. Owlin’s flexible bonuses are checked against the publisher’s Strixhaven preview. Plane Shift and Unearthed Arcana playtest options are not automated.

Other races, backgrounds, multiclass builds and homebrew can use manual entries. Features not explicitly calculated remain subject to the table’s rules and DM decisions.
