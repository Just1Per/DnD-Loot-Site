📖 Magic Item Rarity Progression by Level
Tier 1 Levels 1–4 Local Heroes
Common Uncommon

Players usually find their first Common items early on. Around levels 3–4, characters typically acquire their first Uncommon items — e.g. +1 Weapon, Bag of Holding, Cloak of Protection.

Tier 2 Levels 5–10 Heroes of the Realm
Uncommon Rare

Uncommon items become standard. Around levels 7–9, characters start acquiring Rare items — e.g. +2 Weapon, Flame Tongue, Ring of Protection, Belt of Hill Giant Strength.

Tier 3 Levels 11–16 Masters of the Realm
Rare Very Rare

Rare items are common across the party. Around levels 11–13, characters begin finding Very Rare items — e.g. +3 Weapon, Staff of Power, Manual of Gainful Exercise.

Tier 4 Levels 17–20 Masters of the World
Very Rare Legendary

Very Rare items are standard equipment. Around levels 17+, characters earn Legendary items and Artifacts — e.g. Vorpal Sword, Holy Avenger, Staff of the Magi.
