# Monk Ki and Focus Points

Combat now shows one shared Monk point pool from Monk class level 2: Ki Points for 2014 and Focus Points for 2024. The maximum follows actual Monk levels; the feature save DC follows total character proficiency and current Wisdom. No extra points come from multiclass levels.

Spend 1 point and Undo 1 point apply the selected feature's cost to this one pool. They stop at the limits. From Monk 14, Reroll failed save spends one point from that same pool for Diamond Soul/Disciplined Survivor and reminds the player to roll again and keep the new result. It does not permanently change saving throw modifiers or roll a die automatically.

Short or Long Rest restores Monk points. The 2014 instructions require at least 30 minutes of meditation during the rest. A mixed Artificer/Monk keeps Flash of Genius independent: short rest restores Monk points, while Flash recovers on long rest. The panel's rest buttons do not heal or restore spell slots.

Overview and print show current points, the correct recovery and save DC. The Guide and the existing section help explain the workflow. Spending survives save/reopen and uses existing class-choice fields. Each rules edition has its own saved pool. Changing class, edition or level preserves inactive spending; it does not silently reset those choices. Undo can correct an over-spent saved pool after a level reduction.

The Ki/Focus feature remains partial in the wider audit: individual technique actions, Uncanny Metabolism, Perfect Focus, and other special recovery or subclass exceptions still need their own controls. Do not read a shared point counter as completion of all Monk features.

Tests cover levels 1–20 in both editions, finite spending, rest timing, multiclass maximum/DC, level reduction, independent Artificer recovery, duplicate assignments and save-envelope preservation. Browser checks exercise actual buttons, exhaustion, save/reopen, Overview/print, shared reroll spending/focus, separate editions, mobile touch targets and accessibility. Existing Artificer tests verify the resource-panel refactor.
