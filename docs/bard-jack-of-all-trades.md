# Bard: Jack of All Trades

At actual Bard class level 2, untrained skill checks automatically receive half the proficiency bonus, rounded down. Total character level determines proficiency bonus; unassigned or other class levels do not unlock the feature. Proficiency and Expertise keep their higher multiplier. Manual training and skill adjustments are preserved.

The 2014 feature also contributes to character initiative unless proficiency is already included by another supported source, such as Harengon or 2024 Alert. A flat 2014 Alert bonus still stacks. The 2024 feature applies only to skill checks and adds nothing to initiative. Saving throws and attacks receive no half proficiency.

Abilities & Skills shows automatic Half circles, their source and an explanation. Overview and print show the same totals. Wild Shape skill calculations also inherit the half contribution and round it down, including when proficiency is odd. Other form checks remain manual under the existing companion system. For 2014 untrained tool/other ability checks outside the listed skills and character initiative, apply the benefit manually; the sheet does not resolve those rolls.

Changing class/edition recalculates without rewriting manual ranks. There are no new saved fields, Firestore/Storage rules or migration requirements.

Validation covers actual Bard thresholds, multiclass/total-level caps, odd proficiency rounding, manual/automatic proficiency and Expertise nonstacking, initiative editions/PB-source conflicts, saving throws, UI automatic circles and source labels, save/reopen, skill print and Wild Shape live/printed totals.

Sources: [2014 Basic Rules classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) and [2024 Basic Rules classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes).
