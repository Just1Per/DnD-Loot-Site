# Rule automation expansion — 23–24 November 2026

Source: locally pinned 5etools revision `b9061583536101068b3a59d27e886d1fa664e366`. Generators export names, source identity, bounded choices and mechanical facts. Full rule entries and copied source prose are excluded.

## Species and free casting

Every unlocked racial spell whose declared usage is once per Long Rest now has an independent use/undo counter and Long Rest recovery. Spending does not consume a spell slot. Character-level gates, chosen casting ability and source identity remain separate from class level and class resources. Qualified spell names such as Nondetection (no material component) resolve to the catalogue while preserving the restriction in the action notes. Legacy Tiefling Hellish Rebuke retains its second-level free casting. Flamekin and Rimekin gain their fixed source cantrips and level-3/5 spells; only the Rimekin racial Flame Blade deals Cold damage.

Twenty-two source species variants now have 34 reviewed non-spell resource counters, with checked pinned feature identities and authored short instructions. Their pools follow character level and current Proficiency Bonus, independently of class allocation. Legacy Dragonborn breath uses Short/Long Rest; Fizban/revised breath uses PB per Long Rest with separate level-5 powers. Revised Orc Adrenaline Rush recovers on Short/Long Rest; its Relentless Endurance still needs Long Rest, as does MPMM Adrenaline Rush. Daily/turn keys remain separate across species changes and recover even while deselected. Invalid spent records fail closed, and full-ledger spending is atomic.

Giant Ancestry and Shifting show only their chosen option and cannot record a use before completing that option. EFA Shifter now has its own four source-backed form choices in the builder; these were previously absent. Eladrin exposes the existing INT/WIS/CHA feature-ability choice, supplying its own Fey Step DC. Fury of the Small enforces one spend per turn separately from its daily pool. New turn explicitly clears only that turn limit, including when another creature’s turn begins; it never restores daily uses.

These controls say Record use: they do not assume that a Relentless Endurance trigger occurred, award recipient HP, activate flight/immunity/AC, teleport a creature or establish a temporary form. Those activation and recipient controls remain distinct automation work. `scripts/build-species-resource-data.py` checks authored feature identities against the pinned species/subspecies and exports only names, page/source, numeric resource facts and our own instructions. The audit inventories these 34 counter adapters separately from all 105 species’ remaining effects.

## Feats

New structured facts cover fixed training/expertise, resistance, damage/condition immunity and senses. Damage-resistance choices have bounded selectors, reject duplicates and remain inactive until valid. Bonus Darkvision extends the final species/class range. Only fixed facts from an unambiguous proficiency package are applied; source alternatives are not combined.

82 feats with spell-grant adapters have fixed and/or selected spell references. 29 feats include source-filtered choices for spell level, class list, school, ritual or attack requirements. Examples include Fey Touched, Shadow Touched, Artificer Initiate, Wood Elf Magic and Aberrant Dragonmark. Complete ability-score and casting-ability choices in Feats. Fixed spells and valid choices appear as separate spell-sheet/attack sources, without using a class learning/preparation allowance. Free castings have separate counters. Fey Teleportation and Aberrant Dragonmark recover on Short or Long Rest; ordinary daily grants recover on Long Rest. Unknown shared pools and alternative packages are not combined or converted into invented uses.

Alternative packages now include Strixhaven colleges, High Sorcery moons, Spell Sniper lists, Scion planes, Siberys spell selection, Magic Connoisseur and conditional replacement cantrips. One package is active at a time. Scion resistance stays tied to its cantrip; replacement cantrips need explicit confirmation of the already-known cantrip. Magic Connoisseur uses the actual completed 2024 Magic Initiate list and ability. Siberys suggestions do not require owning a dragonmark; its one free casting recovers on Short or Long Rest.

The resulting export has 1,253 relevant items, 230 base items and all 230 generic templates. It retains child overrides, parent requirements, source edition and inherited casting family. It never imports parent rule entries. A new or unsupported mechanical copy modification fails generation rather than silently changing a fact.

## Spell actions

A reviewed numeric override set now fills 38 further missing action formulas, initially bringing the catalogue to 241 computed damage references. It covers direct spells such as Catapult, Arms of Hadar, Frost Fingers, Snowball Swarm, gravity spells and Psychic Lance. Crown of Stars and Minute Meteors scale their projectile counts while retaining per-projectile damage. Remaining projectile uses are tracked explicitly at the table.

Ice Storm and Flame Strike now show both damage types and their edition-specific slot scaling. Contact Other Plane displays the caster’s fixed DC 15 Intelligence save; Synaptic Static’s concentration penalty no longer creates a second Constitution save in the spell’s primary-save field. Damage/area short descriptions retain these distinctions.

The pinned class-source lookup stores some original supplement lists under `classVariant`. The catalogue now includes those class memberships only when the spell’s own source defines them: 122 spell records are corrected. For example, 2014 Ice Knife and Synaptic Static return to their real class lists. Unrelated optional expansions, such as Tasha’s additions to a PHB spell, are not imported through this rule; campaign edition filters are retained. [Provenance ledger](spell-class-list-audit.json). `build-rules-catalog.py --spells-only` regenerates spell/catalogue outputs without replacing independently maintained feat references.

Compound damage now retains each component’s real type. A type-specific bonus, such as Cold Elemental Affinity, applies to the matching component; a general one-roll bonus appears only once with an instruction to assign its component. Unresolved damage-type choices wait for that choice before applying type-dependent bonuses. Explicit Psychic conversion and Rimekin’s Cold conversion are resolved before eligibility for elemental bonuses.

The paper uses separate compact dice/type fields and short save labels, while keeping full human-readable source details in the attack information. Spell-attack proficiency circles are marked correctly. Manual hit/DC/dice/damage adjustments update the compact fields too; the browser checks actual text fit and an exported PDF.

Saved class spell actions are checked against the current class level, source list and edition. Losing a class/feat/list grant disables the action without deleting its saved spell. Explicit Additional spells and unassigned legacy references remain available through their intended manual source. Access is calculated once per action pass and shared with granted-spell resolution.

Ice Knife shows its separate piercing hit and Cold explosion, with the explosion's own upcasting, save DC and target-area reminder. Flame Blade uses the correct edition-specific scaling and the revised spellcasting damage modifier. Chromatic Orb and Chaos Bolt retain their damage-type choice instructions and slot scaling. Available area facts appear beside action notes. A reference never spends a slot, chooses targets or applies damage to another character.

Sixteen more edition-specific records now have reviewed formulas, bringing the total to 257 and leaving 130 manual damage references. Legacy Magic Missile has automatic per-dart damage/counts. Ray of Sickness keeps its hit roll separate from the legacy Constitution rider save. Antagonize, legacy Conjure Barrage, Dragon’s Breath, both Cordon of Arrows versions, legacy Phantasmal Force, Spray of Cards and Caustic Brew keep their actual dice, upcasting and event/recipient instructions. Cordon upcasting adds planted ammunition, not damage per projectile.

Toll the Dead, Witch Bolt and Hunger of Hadar now require an explicit spell-effect choice. Native attack-name submenus and the shared attack editor use the same saved bounded choice. Toll scales d8/d12 from total character level after choosing full/missing target HP. Witch Bolt separates its scaling initial attack from the fixed automatic 1d12 ongoing event and preserves the Action/Bonus Action and initial-hit differences between editions. Missing, unrelated or stale choices remain unavailable without deleting the reference. Choosing a reference does not cast, spend a resource, create a target or establish Concentration.

The catalogue loader now pins the exact generated release to the deployed code. A matching Firestore release still uses checked parts; a different release is skipped before reading parts and the matching bundled JSON is fetched with its content hash in the URL. Both sources are checksum-verified. A mismatched bundle fails clearly and can retry instead of silently using stale mechanics. This makes new formulas and class-list corrections available without a production catalogue import. `build-rules-catalog.py --spells-only` regenerates the catalogue, manifest and loader release pin together.

## Coverage and remaining work

[The reproducible audit](rule-automation-audit.md) inventories all 105 selectable species, 302 feats, 969 spells and 186 subclasses. All 186 subclass adapters exist. Their retained `partial-mechanics` status describes explicit table decisions and is now accompanied by a separate implementation status/reason.

This delivery does not close all rule automation. The inventory now has an adapter for every identified feat spell-grant group. It still identifies 130 damage references without an action formula, race/feat effects needing individual activation review, and item metadata whose conditional scope has no reviewed automatic adapter. These are distinct from missing source descriptions or missing subclass adapters. Next work should review remaining source-dependent spell packages, individual compound or weapon-dependent spell formulas, and explicit spell/item activation and duration controls. Keep conditional powers tied to deliberate controls rather than granting permanent buffs.
