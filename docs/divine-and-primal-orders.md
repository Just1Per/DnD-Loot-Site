# Divine Order and Primal Order

The 2024 Cleric and Druid now choose their level-1 Order in Character Builder. Overview notices link to a missing choice. The selected role shows its effects beside the field, in Overview and on printed Skills pages.

| Class | Order | Automatic benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cleric | Protector | Martial weapon proficiency and Heavy armor training |
| Cleric | Thaumaturge | One extra Cleric cantrip; Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) on Intelligence (Arcana and Religion) checks |
| Druid | Warden | Martial weapon proficiency and Medium armor training |
| Druid | Magician | One extra Druid cantrip; Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) on Intelligence (Arcana and Nature) checks |

Choose the additional cantrip in the existing Spells builder. Its allowance and validation include the Order choice. Changing to a martial role retains existing spells and removes the extra allowance, so the normal spell builder flags excess choices rather than deleting spells.

The skill bonus does not grant proficiency or Expertise. It combines with existing training and manual adjustments, recalculates from current Wisdom, and is labelled beside the skill total. Both separately named Orders can contribute to Arcana on a Cleric/Druid multiclass. The bonus applies to the displayed Intelligence checks; other ability/skill combinations are not a new general roll calculator.

Actual class levels activate each role; overflow assignments and duplicate class rows do not multiply the derived Order bonus. Starting saving throw proficiencies remain separate. The 2014 classes do not acquire these 2024 features. Existing class-choice storage and the save envelope preserve choices after class/edition changes.

Tests cover both classes at levels 1–20, edition gating, minimum/current Wisdom, training plus manual bonuses, equipment grants, cantrip allowances, multiclass combination/caps, inactive choices and save-envelope parity. Browser checks use the real fields, spell builder, source labels, save/reopen, Overview, print, 320/390px layouts, 44px mobile selectors and axe accessibility.

Source: [2024 Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes), Divine Order and Primal Order. Original UI summaries of mechanical facts.

No Firestore/Storage rule changes, deployment or data migration.
