# Rogue subclass mechanics

All 15 selectable archetypes were reviewed through level 20 against pinned `class/class-rogue.json`, revision `b9061583536101068b3a59d27e886d1fa664e366`.

Arcane Trickster uses native INT Wizard-list profiles, required Mage Hand, two/three additional cantrips, known/prepared allowances, legacy school restrictions, source-level slots and a timed stolen-spell choice. Soulknife owns its own edition-specific Psi dice, free first Whispers, paid later uses, recovery and Veil/Rend recharge, plus calculated primary/Bonus Action Psychic Blade references. Success-only Knack/Homing payments are explicitly labeled and made after confirming success.

Phantom has actual soul-trinket acquisition/destruction, capacity, rest/Initiative recovery, Ghost Walk payment/flight and edition-specific Wails uses. Soul trinkets are held inventory resources rather than a freely refreshed rest pool. Scion has deity resistance/cantrip choices, INT Bloodthirst uses and late partial recovery. Scout supplies Nature/Survival Expertise and movement; Thief supplies climb and revised attunement; Swashbuckler supplies Initiative. Mastermind choices grant tools/languages; Inquisitive tracks its conditional state and limited eye uses. Assassin preserves first-round/surprise edition differences.

Concise instructions cover Sneak Attack eligibility, target-based conditions, reactions, disguises, magical-item/scroll requirements, time windows and ally effects. These remain table decisions, so the audit retains partial-mechanics status. Spell-specific grant abilities are preserved in attack references, including Phantom’s Constitution Augury.

Tests cover every edition/level boundary, multiclass scoping, spellcasting/slots, independent Psi pools, free/paid powers, recharge, trinkets, training, movement, attunement and source-specific choices. Browser coverage exercises trinket-paid Ghost Walk and explicit ending.
