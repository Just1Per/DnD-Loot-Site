Player recap · Session 12

The lanterns beneath Blackwater

From Elara’s notebook · Written by Sophie

There were seven lights beneath the bridge. I counted them twice, because Thorin insisted there were six and a reflection.

When the ferryman finally spoke, he asked for neither gold nor passage. He wanted us to remember a name.

What we learned

The river spirit answers to Ilyra. Mara’s compass points toward her, not north.

Remember this

We promised to return before the first frost.

For next time

Ask Mara why she never mentioned the river.
