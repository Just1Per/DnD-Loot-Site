# Pinned character mechanics expansion

Source: `5etools-v1`, revision `b9061583536101068b3a59d27e886d1fa664e366`. `scripts/build-origin-mechanics.py` emits structured facts, source identities and numeric prerequisites only; source prose and Acrobat code are excluded.

## Origins

Source-matched expanded backgrounds now expose supported fixed/choice tool and language facts through the shared builder and Adobe origins popup. Restricted additional skill choices have actual selectors and validate against the background pool; duplicate, stale and out-of-pool selections remain inert. Alternative complete proficiency packages that cannot be represented independently remain manual rather than granting their combined benefits. The existing equipment preview/add flow remains explicit; no items or coins are added merely by changing a background.

Pallid Elf and Lotusden Halfling have source-reviewed Wisdom cantrip/level-3/level-5 spell references. Each unlocked leveled free casting has its own use tracker and long-rest recovery, independent of ordinary spell slots. Chromatic, Gem and Metallic Dragonborn ancestry choices grant exactly the matching resistance. Other conditional species features remain explicit source reminders.

New choices use the existing private build/Adobe envelopes. Standalone and browser engines both load the expanded catalogues. Changing origins removes derived grants while retaining saved choices and counters.

## Legacy class options

The 2014 invocation catalogue expands from 32 to all 54 pinned records, plus Pact of the Talisman. Warlock-level, known-spell and pact requirements govern activation; Hex/curse eligibility requires table review. Revised 2024 options remain separate. New original concise reminders print as traits. Eldritch Mind grants Concentration advantage, Gift of the Depths grants swimming speed equal to walking speed, and bounded use pools recover only on their declared rest. Targeting, familiar commands, curse state and designated pact-weapon bonuses remain explicit table instructions rather than unconditionally applying to every attack/item.

Legacy Fighter/Paladin/Ranger style pools include their Tasha's options. Blessed/Druidic Warrior choose two eligible cantrips with Charisma/Wisdom class casting statistics. Blind Fighting adds 10-ft Blindsight, thrown-mode weapon attacks receive the +2 damage, and Unarmed Fighting uses d6/d8 according to held equipment without reducing a larger racial/Monk die. Superior Technique has a maneuver choice and an additional tracked die; apply its chosen maneuver and existing superiority die size at the table. Interception is a conditional reaction reminder.

Validation includes source counts/prerequisites, exact choice grants, removal/level/edition/multiclass boundaries, free-use recovery, derived attacks, Adobe origins controls, private save/reopen and retained stock/HP/coins/slots.
