# Weapon training and mastery

Abilities & Skills has a Weapon training & mastery section. Overview links to it and flags incomplete choices. Weapon Master feat entries also link directly to their choices. Save sheet retains selections in the existing Adobe `classFeatureChoices` envelope; no stored schema, rules or migration changes are required.

| 2024 class | Weapon Mastery choices | Restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian | 2 at class level 1, 3 at 4, 4 at 10 | Proficient Simple/Martial melee weapons |
| Fighter | 3 at 1, 4 at 4, 5 at 10, 6 at 16 | Proficient Simple/Martial weapons |
| Paladin, Ranger, Rogue | 2 at 1 | Proficient weapons with mastery properties |

Each class uses its actual assigned level, capped at total character level. Unassigned levels do not increase allowances. Multiple class/feat groups share the selected mastery set; duplicate choices do not grant extra effects. Disabled choices explain missing proficiency or another source already selecting that weapon. Manual weapon proficiency notes accept separate comma, semicolon or newline-delimited names; automatic class/species/background/feat training also qualifies.

The **2024 Weapon Master feat** supplies one eligible weapon mastery. The **2014 Weapon Master feat** supplies proficiency in four Simple/Martial weapon kinds, without granting mastery. It keeps this behavior even when a campaign allows a legacy feat on a 2024 sheet. The feat must meet its eligibility conditions; missing ability-increase choices still have their existing separate reminders.

Selection does not equip or create an item. It unlocks the property for a matching 2024 weapon kind. Overview lists the choices, Skills print lists property/source, and weapon attacks explicitly distinguish selected/unselected mastery. A weapon explicitly using 2014 statistics has no mastery property. Rules for replacing selections (usually after a Long Rest) remain the player's responsibility. The application does not resolve target conditions, extra attacks or damage from mastery properties.

Changing edition/class/level or removing a feat makes unavailable choices inactive without erasing them. Explicitly editing a group replaces saved overflow beyond the current allowance. A choice may not duplicate or silently invalidate another valid choice, including mastery depending on a selected legacy weapon proficiency. Clear/change the dependent mastery first. Manual training is never overwritten.

Source: [2024 Basic Rules class features and progression tables](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes), SRD 5.1/5.2.1 equipment metadata, and the edition-specific Weapon Master feat references already bundled in the catalogue. Short property reminders are explanatory summaries; the table resolves conditional effects.

Engine tests cover both editions, class scaling and multiclass caps, proficiency pools, Barbarian melee restriction, duplicate/forged/inactive choices, feat removal/eligibility, dependency replacement and weapon attack property notes. Browser checks cover selection, saving/reopening, Overview/attack/print, keyboard access, narrow screens and WCAG.
