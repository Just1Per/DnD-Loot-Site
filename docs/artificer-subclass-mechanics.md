# Artificer specialist mechanics

All ten selectable specialists were reviewed at levels 3–20 against pinned `class/class-artificer.json`, revision `b9061583536101068b3a59d27e886d1fa664e366`, with companion metadata checked in pinned TCE/EFA/RHW bestiary and object files. The selected Artificer version gates the specialist. Legacy Tasha specialists remain compatible with a 2024 sheet using the Tasha class; they do not acquire Eberron specialist effects.

Alchemist tracks actual held vials, free rolled creations, paid chosen creations, consumption, source-specific effect scaling, self elixir states and restoration counters. Armorer binds an owned suit, gates its model benefits on wearing it, removes Strength speed penalties, calculates model weapon attacks and tracks field/growth/flight/pull resources. Defensive Field HP is reconciled when armor is doffed. Artillerist tracks cannon creation/capacity and per-slot payment, source-specific Protector/detonation rolls, firearm damage and active counts. Battle Smith has magic-weapon-only INT, derived Defender stats/Repair and shared once-per-turn Jolt. Cartographer has carried-map state, Faerie Fire uses, precision, own map survival and free pathfinding. Reanimator gates modifications, derives companion stats/movement, tracks revival/summon uses and Life Transfer.

All fixed specialist spells are additional grants outside preparation. Focus-dependent spell bonuses require the explicit focus choice. Numeric companion references retain actual class-level, ability and source distinctions. Companion current HP, commands, positions, target restrictions, armor infusion/replication allocation, map recipients and timed spell/elixir outcomes remain table decisions with short original instructions. Audit status remains partial mechanics for those boundaries.

Regression tests cover every source/level boundary, legacy-on-revised compatibility, real elixir inventory/payment, exact owned armor and mixed-case IDs, growth, cannon limits, Defender differences, magic-only weapons, map consumption, modifications, spell grants and field removal. Browser coverage exercises Eberron vial creation/consumption and self flight.
